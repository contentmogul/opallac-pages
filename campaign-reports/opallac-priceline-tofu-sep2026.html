Takeaway & Recommendation

Creative 01 carried the campaign — 96% of spend and 98% of clicks, at the strongest CTR of the set (3.64%, more than double Creative 02's 1.77%). Meta's own delivery optimization concentrated budget behind it within the first few days, which is exactly what a Traffic-objective auction is supposed to do when one asset clearly outperforms.

Recommendation: treat Creative 01's angle/format as the proven direction for this audience and prioritise it — and 1–2 close variants — in the next flight. Creatives 02–03 saw too little spend to call conclusively; a longer, better-funded test would be needed before judging them. Creatives 04–05 saw minimal delivery and are reasonable candidates to retire or replace with fresh executions next round.
